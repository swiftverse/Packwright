Packwright

Turn scattered notes into a context pack you can reuse.

Fill in the boxes that matter and leave the rest blank. Packwright builds a clean Markdown file you can copy or download and paste into Claude or another assistant. It stays in this browser tab. It does not call an assistant, and it does not upload your notes.

About this tool

Packwright is a local form for people who want to hand an assistant a clear brief. You write the goal, who it is for, what you already decided, the limits, the open questions, and the notes you actually have. The file keeps those pieces separate so an assistant can follow the task without treating your notes as checked facts.

There is no account and no quality score. The checklist only tells you if a few essentials are still empty. Word and character counts are not token counts. Assistants count tokens in their own way, and this tool does not guess.

Saving a draft is off until you turn it on. A draft sits in this browser’s ordinary storage on this device. Packwright does not encrypt it, and it does not send it anywhere. Anyone who can use this browser profile can read it. Your browser may also keep its own form history. Downloaded files and anything you paste into an assistant leave this page under your control.

Do not put passwords, payment numbers, or other secrets in a pack. Nothing here hides them.

Draft on this device

Saving a draft is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, this browser keeps a copy of the pack in local storage on this device only. Packwright does not encrypt that copy and does not send it anywhere. Anyone who can open this browser profile can read it. Turn saving off to delete the copy. Closing the tab without saving a draft, and without downloading, drops the notes in this tab.
